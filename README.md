📊 Project Overview – Uber Stock Market Analysis

I developed an interactive Uber Stock Market Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to explore and understand the historical performance of Uber's stock market data. The project focuses on analyzing stock price movements, trading activity, volatility, and relationships between different price indicators over time.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Uber's Open, High, Low, and Close prices, allowing users to observe how the stock price changed across different periods. I also analyzed daily and annual trading volumes to understand variations in market activity and identify periods with unusually high or low trading activity.

To identify the underlying direction of the stock, I incorporated Moving Average analysis, which helps smooth short-term fluctuations and makes long-term price trends easier to interpret. The project also includes year-wise volatility analysis to examine how the level of price fluctuation changed over different years.

Another important part of the project is correlation analysis, where I examined the relationship between opening and closing prices. The dashboard also compares highest and lowest prices by date, providing a clearer understanding of Uber's price range and market movements.

The dashboard includes interactive KPI cards, time-series charts, trend analysis, volume analysis, correlation plots, and date filters with Year, Quarter, Month, and Day levels. These interactive features allow users to explore the stock data from different time perspectives and gain meaningful insights from the dataset.

Overall, this project helped me strengthen my practical skills in Power BI, DAX, Power Query, data visualization, time-series analysis, financial data analysis, and business-oriented storytelling with data.
